FAQ

Important details before a project starts

These answers help set expectations around package scope, Maya readiness, project timelines, and how custom requirements are handled.

Does ₱5,000 include everything?

No. It is a starter package for simple website needs. Additional pages, custom visuals, payment setup, automation, and broader scope require a higher quotation.

Is Maya approval guaranteed?

No. Intellium Digital can prepare the website and customer flow to be Maya Checkout-ready, but final approval still depends on Maya and its business verification requirements.

Are domain, hosting, and ads included?

Only if they are clearly included in the approved proposal. Domain fees, hosting fees, and ad budgets are usually billed separately.

Can I request a custom budget?

Yes. Share your available budget and business goals, and we will recommend the strongest possible scope within that range.

How long does a project take?

Timeline depends on the scope, content readiness, revisions, and integrations. Simpler websites move faster than larger builds or custom app work.
